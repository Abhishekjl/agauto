AgAuto — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

AgAuto is a Chrome extension that helps users automatically fill web forms using their resume or profile. This policy explains what data the extension handles and how.

Data We Do Not Collect

AgAuto has no servers and collects no data. We do not receive, store, or process any information about you, your browsing activity, or the forms you fill.

Data Stored Locally on Your Device

  • Your profile text and uploaded resume/document
  • Your goal/instructions and job description text
  • Extension settings (API key, model name, endpoint URL)
  • A local history of past form fill sessions

All of the above is stored exclusively in Chrome's local extension storage on your own device. It is never transmitted to AgAuto or any third party, except as described below.

AI Model API Calls

When you trigger a form fill, AgAuto sends the following data directly from your browser to the AI model API endpoint you configure in Settings (for example, OpenAI's API):

  • Your profile text
  • A description of the form fields visible on the current page
  • Any job description text you have provided

This network call goes directly from your browser to your chosen provider — AgAuto is not involved. Please review the privacy policy of the AI provider you choose to use (e.g., OpenAI Privacy Policy).

Permissions Explained

  • activeTab / scripting — to read form fields and fill them on the page you are viewing.
  • storage / unlimitedStorage — to save your profile and resume locally.
  • sidePanel — to display the AgAuto panel beside your active tab.
  • debugger — to simulate hardware-level mouse events so that React, Vue, and Angular controlled form elements register input correctly. This permission is not used to intercept network traffic or capture any other browser data.

Children's Privacy

AgAuto is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a revised date. Continued use of the extension after changes constitutes acceptance of the new policy.
